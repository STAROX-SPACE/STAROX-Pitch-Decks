### PMX

STAROX is building PMX, an AI-native product execution platform that turns business objectives into requirements, technical plans, engineering tasks, workflows, and shipped products.

### What We Build

#### PMX
- Product Management AI — What to build based on market signals, user feedback, and live data.
- Engineering Management AI — How to build by converting ideas into technical plans and execution workflows.

#### BUILD
- Project Execution AI — Turn ideas into MVPs, products, platforms, and scalable systems.

#### AUTOMATION
- Business Automation — Automate sales, marketing, CRM, email, WhatsApp, operations, and workflows.

#### TEAM
- TeamX — End-to-end product development, MVPs, SaaS platforms, websites, mobile apps, and enterprise solutions.
- EDITS  — AI video editing, content creation, design, branding, and media production.

### Vision

Build the operating system for modern companies where AI manages execution and humans focus on decisions.
